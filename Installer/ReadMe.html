MothBite

MothBite is an overdrive/distortion plug-in from Moth Production, modelling the PedalPCB Dwarven Hammer analog circuit.

This installer lets you choose any combination of:

  • Standalone application — installed to /Applications/Moth Production/MothBite.app
  • VST3 plug-in — installed to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/MothBite.vst3
  • Audio Unit (AU) plug-in — installed to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/MothBite.component

After installing a plug-in format, rescan plug-ins in your DAW if it doesn't appear automatically.
